## Part 2: Discussion – the answers you must give

Write a short discussion (about 1–2 paragraphs per question) in **your own words**, linked to your map and to Sri Lanka tourism.

### 1. Why STDA’s location matters
**Expected type of answer:**  
- Explain why the **physical location** of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (e.g. in Colombo, on a main road, near transport or government) is important.
- Possible points: **access** for visitors and stakeholders; **visibility** and branding; **connectivity** (roads, public transport); proximity to **government**, **airport**, or **tourism businesses**; **symbolic** role as the national tourism authority.

**Example style:**  
“STDA’s location matters because it is easily accessible to industry stakeholders and visitors. Being in [area name] near [e.g. main road/transport] allows the authority to coordinate with government and private sector. The location also gives the organisation visibility and supports its role as the national body for tourism development.”

---

### 2. How geography improves operations island-wide
**Expected type of answer:**  
- Explain how **geography** (where STDA is situated) helps it **operate across the whole island**.
- Possible points: **Central location** (e.g. Colombo) for reaching all regions; **transport links** (roads, airport) for travel to districts; **communication and logistics**; **coordination** with regional offices or partners; **policy and planning** from a hub that can serve the whole country.

**Example style:**  
“Geography improves STDA’s operations island-wide because its central location in [e.g. Colombo] allows staff to travel to all regions efficiently. Good transport links support coordination with regional tourism bodies and stakeholders. This central hub supports planning, policy, and service delivery across Sri Lanka.”
